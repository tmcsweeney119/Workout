Tim · New Orleans · 2026
Progressive Overload
Add 2–5 lbs when you hit the top of a rep range for two consecutive sessions with clean form. Consistent progressive overload over 13 weeks drives lean muscle — not any single workout.
Lean Muscle Nutrition
Eat at a small surplus of 200–300 calories above maintenance with 0.8–1g protein per lb of bodyweight. You cannot build lean muscle in an aggressive deficit. Protein is the most important variable — hit it every day.
Core Integration
Every session ends with a core finisher. These are not optional. A strong, trained core directly improves your bench, row, squat, and deadlift, and protects your lower back under load.
The 4-Day Structure
Upper & Chest and Back & Biceps alternate with Lower Body and Shoulders, Arms & Abs. Each muscle group gets at least 48 hours of recovery — the minimum for lean muscle repair and growth.
Flexibility Is the Program
The pre-workout stretches and full cooldown are mandatory — not supplemental. Skipping them is where athletes get hurt, especially at Phase 3 loads.
Rest Periods
Compound lifts get 75–120 seconds. Isolation and core work gets 30–60 seconds. Respecting rest periods is part of the program — short rests on isolation work maintain the metabolic stress that drives lean muscle.
Sleep & Recovery
8 hours of sleep is the program. Lean muscle is built during sleep, not during the workout. The workout is the stimulus. Sleep is the adaptation. Do not train hard and sleep poorly and expect results.
